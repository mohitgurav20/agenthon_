Engine Protocol

A step-by-step breakdown of how FLUX automates, secures, and tracks your job search.

1

Ingestion & Vectorization

Your journey begins by uploading your unstructured resume (PDF/DOCX) and defining your exact target career parameters through our Conversational Copilot drawer. The system leverages advanced OpenAI embedding models to index your comprehensive skills and work experience into multi-dimensional vectors, saving them securely inside the Supabase pgvector store.

Supabase pgvector OpenAI Embeddings Zod Schema Validation
2

Semantic Match Discovery

The Orchestrator agent crawls corporate ATS boards and platforms (leveraging LinkedIn, Greenhouse, and Lever) on an automated schedule. Rather than relying on simple keyword filters, ResumeVault employs Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) Hybrid Search to query job description vectors against your resume embeddings, ranking matches with true semantic context.

LinkedIn Fast Scraper RRF Hybrid Search Tavily Job Crawler
3

Browser Engine Inception

For high-scoring job positions, the browser launcher spins up a headless Chromium browser instance within a secure local sandbox. The Python browser-use agent mounts your local Chrome profile, natively logs into portals, dynamically analyzes form fields, and inputs your accurate details directly into the ATS database.

Browser-Use Agent Headless Chromium Local MCP Sandbox
4

Anti-Ban Security

To protect candidate accounts from getting flagged by strict B2B job boards, ResumeVault structures all operations through mathematical Token-Bucket Rate Limiters. If the browser faces an advanced CAPTCHA or 2FA verification challenge, the system triggers a resilient pause, alerts you immediately via WhatsApp, and resumes instantly after you manual-solve.

Token-Bucket Limiter Resilient Pauses WhatsApp Webhooks
5

Recruiter Intel Feedback

Once an application is successfully submitted, you are updated instantly. Even better, when a corporate recruiter opens your file or updates your application status, Twilio webhook integration captures the state update, generating a realistic synthesized recruiter voice memo through ElevenLabs to brief you on-demand.

ElevenLabs TTS Voice Twilio Integration Real-time Dashboard Feedback
